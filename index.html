BendoMarket Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

BendoMarket is a mobile marketplace that allows users to explore products and shops, list second-hand items, manage orders, and access seller or shop-owner services.

This Privacy Policy explains how BendoMarket collects, uses, stores, and discloses personal information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BendoMarket, the information collected may include:

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, phone number, physical address, and password.
  • Profile information: information you provide when managing your account, including profile details and, where applicable, a profile image.
  • Order and delivery information: information necessary to manage purchases, orders, and delivery arrangements.
  • Product and listing information: details, descriptions, and images you submit when listing products or second-hand items.
  • Subscription payment information: the payment method selected, the payer's phone number, transaction reference, and uploaded payment receipt when subscribing to a seller or shop-owner plan.
  • Contact information: your name, email address, and message when you contact us through the application's contact form.
  • Support and communications: information you provide when contacting us about an account, order, or other issue.

Please do not include passwords, PIN codes, or other authentication secrets in payment receipts or support messages.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information, as applicable, to:

  • Create and manage user, seller, and shop-owner accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Display and manage product listings and shop information.
  • Process orders and coordinate delivery.
  • Review subscription payments and manage subscriptions.
  • Respond to questions, contact requests, and support messages.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application's services.
  • Address security issues, fraud, disputes, and legal obligations.

3. Payments

BendoMarket displays payment options that include MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and cash on delivery. Subscription registration may also require a payer phone number, transaction reference, and payment receipt.

Payment information is used for the relevant transaction, payment verification, order fulfilment, or subscription administration. The exact information processed by payment providers depends on the payment method and the transaction.

BendoMarket does not request your mobile-money PIN through the registration form. Never share your PIN with us.

4. When Information May Be Shared

Personal information may need to be disclosed to relevant parties to operate the marketplace, for example:

  • Sellers, shop owners, or delivery personnel, where information is necessary to fulfil an order or arrange delivery.
  • Service providers that host, operate, or support the application, where they process information on our behalf.
  • Payment providers or transaction recipients, where necessary to complete or verify a payment.
  • Public authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by applicable law or is necessary to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only as appropriate for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable law and the actual operation of the service.

5. Data Storage and Security

BendoMarket uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Information may be processed by the application's backend and by providers used to operate the service, such as hosting or image-storage services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including account administration, order fulfilment, payment verification, dispute resolution, security, and applicable legal obligations.

Actual retention periods depend on the type of information and the relevant operational or legal requirements.

7. Account Deletion and Your Choices

You may contact us to request deletion of your BendoMarket account and associated personal information.

To submit a request, email abassledesigner@gmail.com using the email address associated with your account. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line and provide enough information for us to identify your account. Do not send your password or mobile-money PIN.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where necessary to resolve transactions, disputes, or security issues. We will handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

BendoMarket is not intended to be used by children who are not legally permitted to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may rely on third-party services to provide hosting, image storage, communications, payment processing, or other functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

The providers actually used and the information shared with each provider depend on the features you use.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, our practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
